# Billing Safety

Billing safety means I watch spending before it becomes a surprise.

## Simple rules
- Turn on billing alerts.
- Create a low monthly budget.
- Check billing after every lab.
- Stop anything that is not needed.
- Use root only for account-level billing settings, then return to an IAM user.

## Why it matters
AWS is easy to use, and that is good.
It also means it is easy to leave something running and keep paying for it.

## What the billing video showed
- IAM users may not see Billing and Cost Management until root enables IAM and role access to billing information.
- The monthly summary shows current cost, previous-period comparison, and forecast.
- Detailed bills show which service created a charge.
- Service charges can often be expanded by Region and sub-item.
- A zero-spend budget can notify me when spending goes above a very small amount.
- A monthly cost budget can notify me when spending crosses my chosen monthly limit.

## Beginner rule
If I do not understand the cost of a service, I do not create it yet.

## Important warning
Budgets notify me. They do not automatically stop or delete AWS resources.

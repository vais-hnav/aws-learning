# Free Tier Rules

## Free Tier is helpful, not magical
Free Tier can reduce cost, but it does not remove all risk.

## Rules to follow
- Use small, beginner-safe services.
- Read the pricing page before creating anything.
- Stop services when I finish.
- Delete anything I do not need.
- Watch billing every time I learn a new lab.

## Services to avoid for now
- Large EC2 instances
- RDS databases
- NAT Gateway
- Load balancers
- EKS clusters
- OpenSearch
- Redshift

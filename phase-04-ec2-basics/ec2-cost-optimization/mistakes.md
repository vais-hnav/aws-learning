# Mistakes

## Thinking Cheapest Is Always Best

The video warns that savings are not good if they harm customer experience or future innovation.

Better:
- remove waste first
- right-size carefully
- choose cost options based on workload needs

## Buying Commitments Too Early

Savings Plans and Reserved Instances are useful for predictable usage, but they are not beginner lab actions.

## Using Spot For Anything Critical

Spot Instances can be interrupted. Use them only for workloads that can handle interruption.

## Ignoring Idle Resources

The easiest beginner saving is deleting what is unused:
- old EC2 instances
- unused EBS volumes
- unused Elastic IPs
- old snapshots

## Forgetting Budgets Are Not Cleanup

Budgets notify you. They do not automatically delete every resource that causes cost.
